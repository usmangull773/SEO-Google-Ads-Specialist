Where qualified demand is leaking

These are specific, fixable, and none of them need a rebuild. Each one costs you visibility or measurement you are already paying to earn.

The homepage title carries no keywords

Needs fix

Your most important page tells Google almost nothing. No mention of Peppol, e-invoicing or Access Point in the title. This is a fast, high impact correction.

Title: "Home · Arratech AB"

No hreflang across your market pages

Needs fix

You target six countries but the site only declares one language and region. Search engines cannot map the right page to the right market, so priority markets lose visibility.

Declared: en-US only

A dev version of the site is reaching Google

Needs fix

A development subdomain is publicly indexable. That risks duplicate content against your real pages and wastes crawl attention. It should be closed off.

dev.arratech.com appears in search

The main B2B channel is not being measured

Needs fix

No LinkedIn tag was detected. For a long, multi stakeholder B2B journey, that is the one audience layer you most want to see and reach. A blind spot on your best channel.

LinkedIn Insight Tag: not detected

FAQ and product pages miss their structured data

Needs fix

You have an FAQ page, but not the schema that lets Google and AI answer engines surface it. Free real estate in search and AI results is being left on the table.

FAQ and Product schema absent

The order I would work in

Fixes first where the return is fast and provable, then the compounding work. Priorities would move with what the internal data shows.

Step 1Confirm the baseline

Read the internal picture across Search Console, GA4 and the Ads account, then validate these public findings and set the real priorities from evidence.

Step 2Fix the fast leaks

Homepage and key page titles, hreflang across the six markets, close the dev subdomain, and add FAQ and product structured data.

Step 3Make Search measurable

Verify demo and enquiry conversions in GA4 and Ads, add the missing B2B audience layer, and tidy UTM conventions so reporting can be trusted.

Step 4Grow qualified demand

Structure Search around mandate intent, France now, UK and Belgium next, align ads to landing pages, and report on demand and pipeline, not clicks.
